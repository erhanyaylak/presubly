YORUM 1.1 [Strateji: KABUL + REVIZYON]
────────────────────────────────
Reviewer Comment:
"The sample size justification is insufficient. Please
provide a power analysis."

Author Response:
We thank Reviewer 1 for this important observation. We
have now included a post-hoc power analysis (G*Power
3.1, Faul et al., 2009) in the revised Methods section
(p. 6, paragraph 3). With observed effect size d = 0.62
and n = 84, achieved power is 0.87, exceeding the
conventional 0.80 threshold.

Revizyon:
  → Methods, paragraf 3, satir 142-148: Guc analizi
    eklendi.
  → Referanslar: Faul et al. (2009) eklendi.

YORUM 1.2 [Strateji: KABUL + REVIZYON]
────────────────────────────────
Reviewer Comment:
"The allocation concealment method is not described.
Please clarify per CONSORT Item 9."

Author Response:
We agree with this observation and apologize for the
omission. We have added a detailed description of the
allocation concealment procedure to the Methods section.
Specifically, sequentially numbered, opaque, sealed
envelopes (SNOSE) were used, prepared by an independent
statistician not involved in participant recruitment.

Revizyon:
  → Methods, paragraf 4, satir 156-162: Allocation
    concealment proseduru eklendi.

YORUM 1.3 [Strateji: KANITLA ITIRAZ]
────────────────────────────────
Reviewer Comment:
"The AI model seems overtrained. Validation on an
external dataset is needed."

Author Response:
We respectfully disagree with the assessment of
overtraining. Our model demonstrates consistent
performance across both internal validation (AUC = 0.91,
95% CI: 0.87-0.95) and the held-out test set (AUC =
0.88, 95% CI: 0.83-0.93), with a modest and expected
performance gap. Additionally, 5-fold cross-validation
yielded a mean AUC of 0.89 (SD = 0.03), suggesting
robust generalizability. However, we acknowledge the
value of external validation and have added this as a
limitation and future work direction (Discussion, p. 14).

Revizyon:
  → Discussion, paragraf 7: External validation
    sinirlilik olarak eklendi.

YORUM 2.2 [Strateji: KISMI KABUL]
────────────────────────────────
Reviewer Comment:
"The comparison with traditional diagnostic methods is
superficial. A head-to-head comparison table is needed."

Author Response:
We partially agree with this comment. We have added a
comprehensive comparison table (new Table 5) showing
sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, and AUC for our
AI model versus traditional HbA1c-based screening and
clinician judgment. However, a full randomized head-to-
head comparison was beyond the scope of this initial
validation study, which we have clarified in the
Discussion.

Revizyon:

  → Discussion, paragraf 5: Kapsam sinirlamasi

YORUM 3.1 [Strateji: KABUL + REVIZYON]
────────────────────────────────
Reviewer Comment:
"Effect sizes should be reported for all primary
outcomes."

Author Response:
We thank Reviewer 3 for this methodological reminder.
Cohen's d has been calculated and reported for all
primary outcomes in the revised Results section and
Table 4. Effect sizes ranged from d = 0.42 (moderate)
to d = 0.78 (large), supporting clinical significance.

Revizyon:
  → Results, Tablo 4: Cohen's d sutunu eklendi.
  → Results, paragraf 3: Effect size yorumu eklendi.

YORUM 3.2 [Strateji: KABUL + REVIZYON]
────────────────────────────────
Reviewer Comment:
"The limitations section is too brief."

Author Response:
We agree and have substantially expanded the limitations
section to address: (1) single-center design, (2)
absence of external validation cohort, (3) potential
selection bias in recruitment, (4) AI model's dependency
on image quality, and (5) lack of long-term follow-up
data.

Revizyon:
  → Discussion, paragraf 6-7: Sinirliliklar bolumu
    genisletildi (eski 4 satir → yeni 18 satir).

Brand Story
Presubly
AI Pre-Submission Review Platform
01 The Problem
Millions of manuscripts get rejected every year.
Turkey produces over 70,000 academic articles annually. Yet the rejection rate at international journals exceeds 60%. Most rejections stem not from a lack of scientific merit, but from methodological gaps, formatting errors, and non-compliance with reporting standards.
When a researcher submits a manuscript, they wait weeks or months for the outcome. When rejection arrives, the same preventable mistakes repeat at the next journal. This cycle erodes motivation and slows scientific progress.

"My manuscript had two critical CONSORT violations. If the journal reviewer had flagged them first, I would have lost months."

02 Our Vision
Review before rejection.
Presubly reverses the manuscript submission process. Before the paper reaches the journal, AI evaluates it like a real peer reviewer. The goal is simple: eliminate preventable rejections.
We believe every researcher deserves a fair chance at evaluation. But fair evaluation starts with prepared submission. Presubly provides the technological foundation for that preparation.
03 What We Do
Four tools, full preparation.
Presubly offers four integrated tools covering every stage of the pre-submission process:
TOOL 01
Scientific Peer Review
7 academic criteria, 100-point scale. Issues prioritized with Major/Minor/Suggestion labels and Accept/Reject prediction.
TOOL 02
Editorial Assessment
Scope fit, methodology, originality, and ethics analysis from the editor's perspective. Decision notification letter draft.
TOOL 03
Response Assistant
Upload reviewer and editor comments — standards-compliant responses generated for each. Accept, rebut, or partially accept strategies.
TOOL 04
Submission Checklist
Automatic checks against CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA and more. Color-coded results with clear "Submit now / Fix first" decision.
04 Our Standards
Every assessment at global standards.
Presubly converts international academic publishing standards into machine-readable format and automatically applies them in every analysis:
  • COPE — Publication ethics, author contributions, data integrity, review process fairness
  • ICMJE — Author eligibility, conflicts of interest, ethics for medicine and health sciences
  • CONSORT — 25-item checklist for randomized controlled trials
  • STROBE — Standard for observational epidemiological studies
  • PRISMA — Systematic review and meta-analysis standard (27 items)
  • COREQ & ARRIVE — Qualitative research and animal study standards

07 Name & Identity
Why "Presubly"?
Pre (before) + Submission (sending) + ly (adverb suffix) = Presubly. The name carries the meaning of "before submission" and summarizes the platform's core value proposition in a single word.
In the logo, the "sub" portion is highlighted in amber — a direct reference to the submission concept. The teal primary color represents academic seriousness, while amber accents convey energy and action.
Presubly is part of the Tenopark Academic Software Ecosystem. Together with MisanpAIge (typesetting & citations), EthicsKit (ethics), JournalKit (journal management), and CVita (academic CV), it offers an integrated solution covering the entire academic publishing workflow.
